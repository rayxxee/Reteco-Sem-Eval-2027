\section{Feature Engineering}
\label{sec:features}

Informed directly by our Exploratory Data Analysis, we engineered a 13-dimensional feature matrix for every candidate pair $(Q, D)$ within the top-50 candidate pool generated by BM25. The features, summarized in Table~\ref{tab:features}, capture lexical matching, text length dynamics, and explicit temporal grounding:

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\small
\caption{The 13 engineered features utilized by the classical LightGBM reranker.}
\label{tab:features}
\begin{tabular}{lp{4.2cm}p{6.8cm}}
\toprule
\textbf{Feature Name} & \textbf{Feature Group} & \textbf{Mathematical Definition and EDA Rationale} \\
\midrule
\texttt{bm25\_score} & Base Lexical & Raw Okapi BM25 score ($k_1=0.9, b=0.4$). Measures keyword frequency. \\
\texttt{bm25\_norm} & Base Lexical & Min-max normalized BM25 score within candidate window $[0, 1]$. \\
\texttt{tfidf\_cosine} & Base Lexical & Cosine similarity between TF-IDF vector representations of $Q$ and $D$. \\
\texttt{reciprocal\_rank} & Candidate Position & $1 / \text{rank}_{\text{BM25}}$, rewarding candidates retrieved at high initial ranks. \\
\addlinespace
\texttt{jaccard\_overlap} & Vocabulary Overlap & $\frac{|T_Q \cap T_D|}{|T_Q \cup T_D|}$, addressing the low lexical overlap discovered in EDA. \\
\texttt{len\_query} & Length / Structure & Character length of the query string. \\
\texttt{len\_doc} & Length / Structure & Character length of the document text. \\
\texttt{log\_len\_doc} & Length / Structure & $\log(1 + |D|)$, mitigating the heavy right-tail length skew in corpus posts. \\
\texttt{len\_ratio} & Length / Structure & $|Q| / (|D| + 1)$, capturing relative conciseness. \\
\addlinespace
\texttt{has\_year\_query} & Temporal Grounding & Indicator $\mathbf{1}[Y_Q \neq \emptyset]$, detecting presence of 4-digit years in query. \\
\texttt{has\_year\_doc} & Temporal Grounding & Indicator $\mathbf{1}[Y_D \neq \emptyset]$, detecting presence of 4-digit years in passage. \\
\texttt{year\_overlap} & Temporal Alignment & $|Y_Q \cap Y_D|$, counting shared four-digit year mentions. \\
\texttt{year\_overlap\_frac}& Temporal Alignment & $|Y_Q \cap Y_D| / \max(1, |Y_Q|)$, fraction of query years present in document. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\textbf{Connection to EDA:} Feature \texttt{year\_overlap} directly implements the key insight from EDA Section~\ref{sec:eda}: while date mentions are pervasive across both relevant and irrelevant documents, exact \textit{congruence} between the query's target era and the document's timestamp is highly discriminative. Logarithmic document length (\texttt{log\_len\_doc}) counters the extreme right-skewed document tail, preventing long, unfocused forum answers from dominating rankings.
