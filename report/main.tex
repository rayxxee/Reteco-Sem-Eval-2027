\begin{abstract}
This report details our system and experiments for the RETECO SemEval-2027 Task 1 (Temporally Grounded Retrieval), specifically Sub-track 1a (whole-query retrieval). We establish strong classical baselines (BM25, TF-IDF), analyze temporal and lexical features, implement a pointwise LightGBM reranker, and compare performance against a frozen dense retriever (BGE-small). We evaluate on a CPU-friendly subset of the dataset and highlight limitations in semantic mismatch and temporal alignment.
\end{abstract}
