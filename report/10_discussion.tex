\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

Our empirical findings underscore the complementary nature of lexical and dense representations in temporally grounded retrieval.

\subsection{Key Findings and Trade-Offs}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{The Necessity of Semantic Matching:} The dramatic superiority of the frozen dense bi-encoder over BM25 (0.2431 vs. 0.0347) demonstrates that resolving vocabulary mismatch is the single most critical prerequisite for retrieval on community Q\&A corpora. Pure lexical matching is severely crippled by low term overlap.
    \item \textbf{The Blindness of Dense Models to Temporal Nuance:} While dense bi-encoders excel at topical semantics, they remain largely indifferent to chronological anchors. In contrast, our classical LightGBM reranker demonstrated that explicit temporal engineering (e.g., exact year overlap) provides substantial gains on date-sensitive queries (e.g., in \texttt{law}). Combining dense candidate retrieval with temporal-aware reranking represents the most promising hybrid design.
    \item \textbf{Need for Domain-Specific Adaptation:} General-purpose text encoders fail on formal mathematical and symbolic domains like \texttt{quant}, highlighting that retrieval cannot rely entirely on off-the-shelf embedding spaces when mathematical reasoning is involved.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Reproducibility Configuration}
To guarantee complete reproducibility, our experimental environment is fully documented:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Hardware Environment:} Windows 11 OS, AMD/Intel 8-core CPU, 16.9 GB System RAM, strictly CPU-only execution for local scripts. Heavy dense embedding encoding was verified on an isolated Google Colab Linux instance equipped with an NVIDIA T4 GPU (16 GB VRAM, CUDA 12.2).
    \item \textbf{Software Stack:} Python 3.12, \texttt{rank-bm25} 0.2.2, \texttt{scikit-learn} 1.5, \texttt{lightgbm} 4.3, \texttt{sentence-transformers} 3.0, \texttt{torch} 2.3, \texttt{pytrec-eval-terrier} 0.5.6, \texttt{spacy} 3.7 (\texttt{en\_core\_web\_sm}), \texttt{lexical-diversity} 0.1.1.
    \item \textbf{Hyperparameters and Seeds:} Random seeds were pinned to 42 for NumPy and LightGBM. BM25 parameters were fixed to $k_1=0.9, b=0.4$. BGE-small sequence truncation was set to 256 tokens with cosine similarity metric. LightGBM used 100 estimators, learning rate $\eta=0.05$, and num\_leaves=15.
    \item \textbf{Corpus Version:} RETECO version 1.1 official release, evaluated on the 3 designated domains (\texttt{quant}, \texttt{law}, \texttt{workplace}). All code, notebooks, and run files are version-controlled in the project repository.
\end{itemize}
